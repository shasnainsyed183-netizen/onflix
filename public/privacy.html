Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

🔒 Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and profile picture when you sign up.
  • Usage Data: Movies watched, time spent, and interactions with the platform.
  • Device Information: Browser type, device type, and IP address for security purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve our streaming service.
  • To personalize your viewing experience and recommendations.
  • To communicate with you about your account and updates.
  • To protect against fraud and abuse.

3. Google Sign-In

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture. We do not access your Google password or other Google data. You can revoke ONflix's access from your Google account settings at any time.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We use industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

5. Cookies

We use cookies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and analyze site usage. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features may not work properly.

6. Third-Party Services

Videos on ONflix are embedded from platforms like YouTube, Vimeo, and Dailymotion. These platforms may collect their own data according to their privacy policies.

7. Your Rights

  • You can access and update your personal information in your profile settings.
  • You can delete your account by contacting us.
  • You can opt out of non-essential communications.

8. Children's Privacy

ONflix is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us.
